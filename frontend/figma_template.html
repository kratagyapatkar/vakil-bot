What are my fundamental rights if police arrests me in India?
⚖️
📌 Summary & Overview

Under the Constitution of India and the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) 2023, every arrested individual is entitled to mandatory procedural safeguards to protect personal liberty against arbitrary custody.

⚖️ Applicable Laws & Sections
  • Article 22, Constitution of India: Protection against arrest and detention.
  • Section 173 BNSS: Mandatory registration of FIR for cognizable crimes.
  • Section 47 BNSS: Mandatory duty of police to inform grounds of arrest and right to bail.
🛡️ Your Core Constitutional Rights
  • Right to Know Grounds: Immediate notice of charges and offences alleged.
  • Right to Consult an Advocate: Freedom to contact a lawyer of your choice.
  • 24-Hour Magistrate Rule: Must be presented before nearest Magistrate within 24 hours.
  • Free Legal Aid: Access to legal counsel under Article 39A (NALSA).
📞 Immediate Helplines & Portals
  • National Legal Aid (NALSA): 15100 (Free lawyer assistance)
  • Police Control Room: 112 / 100
  • National Women Helpline: 1091
⚠️ Important Disclaimer

This information is for general educational awareness. For criminal proceedings, consult a practicing High Court or Sessions Court advocate immediately.
